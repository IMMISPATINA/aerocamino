About AeroCamino

Navigate your aviation career with confidence.

AeroCamino is a professional aviation mentoring platform designed to help pilots prepare for critical career milestones with structure, clarity and practical insight.

Whether you are preparing for an airline assessment, a command upgrade or an international licensing pathway, AeroCamino focuses on professional preparation grounded in real airline operations and training experience.

Our Services

Focused support for the moments that shape a pilot’s career.

Airline Interview Preparation

Technical, HR and competency-based interview coaching with structured preparation and feedback.

02

Command Upgrade Coaching

Leadership, decision-making, CRM and command assessment preparation for pilots progressing to the left seat.

03

EASA Conversion Guidance

Clear, step-by-step guidance for pilots navigating ICAO-to-EASA licensing and qualification pathways.

04

Pilot Career Mentoring

Personalized career planning, professional positioning and development for aspiring and experienced airline pilots.
